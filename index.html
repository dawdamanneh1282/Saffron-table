Our story

From the kitchen to your table

Saffron Table began as a Sunday lunch tradition — four dishes, one long table, whoever turned up. Ten years later the table is bigger, but the recipes are the same ones written on the back of a notebook in 1994.

We source spices in small batches and roast them the morning they are used. Nothing is frozen, and the dal really does take twelve hours.
